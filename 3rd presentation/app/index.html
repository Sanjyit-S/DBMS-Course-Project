🛡️ Integrity Constraints Defense & Live Negative Tests

Demonstrate to the evaluating faculty that the database actively guards against corrupt or anomalous data at the storage engine level.

1. Negative Baggage Weight

CHECK Constraint

Rule: weight_kg >= 0.00 in baggage table.

2. Double-Booking Physical Seat

UNIQUE Constraint

Rule: UNIQUE (flight_id, seat_id) in tickets table.

INSERT INTO tickets (booking_id, flight_id, seat_id, fare_amount) VALUES (2, 1, 1, 8500.00);

3. Same Airport Flight Route

CHECK Constraint

Rule: origin_airport <> dest_airport in routes table.
