MATHEMATICAL NORMALIZATION PROOF: UNF → 3NF → BCNF

Elimination of Partial & Transitive Dependencies with Lossless Join Decomposition
BCNF COMPLIANT
1. First Normal Form (1NF)
• Eliminated multi-valued attribute arrays (e.g. composite passenger names split into first_name & last_name).
• Every column holds strictly atomic values.
2. Second Normal Form (2NF)
• Eliminated Partial Functional Dependencies where non-prime attributes depended on a subset of composite keys.
• Aircraft model separated from seats: aircraft_id → model.
3. Third Normal Form (3NF)
• Eliminated Transitive Dependencies: X → Y → Z.
• Airports separated from routes so city/country depend directly on airport_code, not transitively on route_id.
4. Boyce-Codd Form (BCNF)
• For every non-trivial functional dependency X → Y, X is strictly a Superkey.
• Preserves lossless join property: R1 ∩ R2 → R1 or R2.

Q: How do you enforce 1:1 vs 1:N in relational schema?
"A foreign key is 1:N by default. To make it strictly 1:1, we place a UNIQUE constraint on the FK column. For example, checkins.ticket_id is marked UNIQUE, guaranteeing at most one check-in per ticket."
Q: Why did we decompose Passengers & Flights?
"Passengers and Flights have a Many-to-Many (M:N) relationship. To satisfy 1NF, we decomposed M:N into two 1:N relationships via the junction entity tickets."
